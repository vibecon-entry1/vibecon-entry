## Provenance & determinism (user decision, recorded)

The "seeded RNG only" determinism rule constrains the RUNTIME sim, not where
a committed PNG came from: shipped assets are static PNGs baked into the
atlas and never regenerated by the game. Art may therefore be sourced by
prompted image generation. What replaces determinism is REPRODUCIBILITY OF
PROCESS: every generation's prompt and raw output are committed under
`assets-wow/raw/<name>/` (prompt.txt + raw_0.jpg), and every derived asset is
a pure function of those raws via `tools/genart_v2_prod.py` (chroma-key
extraction → craft pass → native-res palette-locked pixel art). Rebuild
everything with `python3 tools/genart_v2_prod.py`.

## Real layer geometry (measured from web/game/scenes/play.js)

- Horizon (`restLine` at rest camera): screen y=232; ground = 8 rows of 16px
  tiles from y=232 down.
- par_mesas: 640x120 drawn with `band('par_mesas', .30, .12, 4)` → strip
  bottom at restLine+4 = 236.
- par_rocks: 640x80, `band(..., .60, .20, 10)` → bottom at 242. Far-ground
  haze fill starts at rocksBottom-20 = 222.
- Sky (`par_stars` slot) scrolls at 0.10x and is drawn twice at ±640 →
  **the sky must tile horizontally.** The production skies are uniform per
  row (banded), so they tile perfectly; that is also why the sun ships as a
  separate cameo instead of being baked in. Scroll strategy: unchanged 0.10x
  parallax works as-is; the sun joins the single-placement prop pass
  (play.js SCENERY list) so it parallaxes like a distant object.

## Pipeline changes needed at integration

1. build_assets.py GEN entries: `tiles` frame count 4 → 10 (160x16 strip);
   add `sun` (128x128), `prop_spire`/`prop_arch`/`prop_wreck`, and
   `flora_0..7` cells; `prop1`/`prop2` source PNGs swap from assets-extra to
   the production re-composites (same cell sizes 19x28 / 27x28 — no play.js
   change needed for them).
2. Sky variants: play.js draws one `par_stars` name; boss/wow sky swaps need
   either extra atlas cells + a per-mode name pick, or a palette tint at
   draw time. Cells are ready either way.
3. `tiles_wow` swap in the wow zone mirrors the sky swap decision.
4. Floor depth bands (render step 2b, alphas .15/.30) reproduced in mocks
   over the new tiles — keep them.
5. Flora placement: scatter as scenery (non-colliding) with feet on the
   floor line; mock density ≈ 3-5 pieces per screen, mid-band pieces sit on
   the haze line like the props.
6. Title scene: draw title.png first, keep text/hero layout on top; hero
   staged lower-left in the mock with a soft contact shadow.
